Bond Credit Risk Case Lab

What can cause a bond investment to lose money even when the issuer continues paying?

Purpose

An interactive companion to the UZH Executive Education class Credit Risk Management in Bond Investment (ICBC senior managers and experts, 30 October 2026, 10:00–12:30, consecutive interpretation). It runs the whole class use case and stays available to participants afterwards.

One fictional position — a 3.60% RMB 100 m senior unsecured MTN of Huaxing Development Co., Ltd., bought at issue on 15 March 2025 — is followed through four dated stops. Every number is computed live by a tested engine and the app ends by answering the class question explicitly.

Inputs

  • Case data in data/case.json (fictional teaching assumptions).
  • Presets: Class defaults (locked, reproduces the deck) and Free play (every input editable within bounds).
  • Instructor tallies and participant answers, stored only in the browser.

Outputs

  • Live metric tiles for the selected date and counterfactual.
  • Every calculation with “Show workings”: formula and inputs.
  • Printable case fact sheet and decision record; decision export/import as JSON.

Running offline and hosted

  • app/index.html is a single self-contained file: open it from file:// with no internet.
  • The same file is deployed to GitHub Pages and to the portfolio path /risk-intelligence/bond-credit-risk-case-lab/app/.

Privacy

No backend, no accounts, no analytics and no network calls. State stays in this browser’s localStorage.

债券信用风险案例实验室

即使发行人持续付息，债券投资为何仍可能亏损？

用途

苏黎世大学高管教育课程“债券投资中的信用风险管理”的互动配套工具（面向中国工商银行高级管理人员和专家，2026年10月30日，10:00–12:30，交替传译）。它贯穿整个课堂案例，课后学员仍可继续使用。

本应用跟踪一个虚构头寸——华兴发展有限公司发行的票面利率3.60%、面值1亿元的优先无担保中期票据，于2025年3月15日在发行时买入——历经四个带日期的节点。所有数字均由经过测试的计算引擎实时计算，应用最后明确回答课程的核心问题。

输入

  • 案例数据位于 data/case.json（虚构的教学假设）。
  • 预设：“课堂默认值”（锁定，复现课堂讲义）和“自由模式”（所有输入均可在范围内编辑）。
  • 讲师计票和学员答案，仅保存在浏览器中。

输出

  • 所选日期和反事实情景的实时指标卡。
  • 每项计算均可通过“显示计算过程”查看公式和输入。
  • 可打印的案例概况表和决策记录；决策可导出/导入为JSON。

离线运行与在线托管
